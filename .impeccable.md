# Design context: supplier inquiry and project cost tool

## Users and setting
- Procurement, engineering and project staff at one company. At most 30 people and 90 devices.
- Everyone works in Chinese.
- **Windows desktop is where most of the work happens:** keyboard, mouse, large screens, long sessions entering numbers into tables.
- **Android** is used on site and in meetings: look things up and enter single records.
- **macOS** is a secondary desktop.
- Typical jobs:
  - record a supplier quote against a project;
  - find the cheapest valid quote for a material;
  - turn a customer's equipment list into a project budget;
  - export the quote sheet, cost budget and inquiry list to Excel;
  - exchange data files between devices once a week.
- They know Excel well. The app must never feel slower or less exact than a spreadsheet.

## Personality
Precise, trustworthy, quiet. It should feel like a well-kept ledger, not a startup dashboard.
- Numbers are the protagonists.
- Chrome recedes.
- Colour carries meaning, not decoration.

## Aesthetic direction: "engineering ledger"
- Warm paper-toned canvas, ink-dark text and hairline rules, like a printed procurement ledger.
- One accent: **verdigris (铜绿) green-teal** for primary actions and selection. It is industrial and calm, and distinct from warning amber and error vermilion.
- **Solid versus dashed carries meaning across the whole app:**
  - solid = confirmed data;
  - dashed = proposal not yet confirmed (AI matches, import previews, unsaved edits).

  The user always knows what is real.
- Tables before cards. Grouped ledgers with subtotal rules, not tiles.

## Typography
- **Latin and numerals:** IBM Plex Sans, with tabular figures for every amount and quantity.
- **Codes and model numbers:** IBM Plex Mono.
- **Chinese:** the platform UI face (Microsoft YaHei UI / PingFang SC / Noto Sans SC). It renders best natively and avoids shipping 20 MB of CJK fonts.
- Plex fonts are OFL-licensed and bundled with the app, about 400 KB.
- **Scale (px):** 12 caption, 13 table, 14 body, 16 section, 20 page title, 28 key figure. Weights 400 and 600 only.

## Principles
1. **Exactness is visible.** Right-aligned tabular numbers, currency and tax mode always shown next to totals, and never a rounded value where an exact one exists.
2. **Three steps or fewer** for every core task, a rule borrowed from the project-management analysis.
3. **Confirm before commit.** Anything produced by AI or an import is shown dashed, with counts of what needs attention, until the user confirms.
4. **Keyboard first on desktop.** Tab/Enter move through table cells, Ctrl+N creates, / focuses search, Esc closes panels.
5. **Touch first on Android.** 48dp targets, bottom navigation, one task per screen, totals pinned at the bottom.
6. **No jargon.** Never show UUIDs, versions, hashes or device IDs outside the "Diagnostics" section.

## Anti-goals
- Hero metric cards, gradient headers, glassmorphism, and purple for AI.
- Card grids for lists.
- Colour used only for decoration.
- Modal dialogs stacked on modal dialogs.
- Emoji.

## Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Visible focus rings.
- Colour is never the only signal: warnings pair an icon with a word.
- Honour OS text scaling up to 130% without clipping.
